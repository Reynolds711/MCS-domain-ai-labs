Lab 301-3: Adaptive Tutor Agent

🎓 Lab 301-3: Build an Adaptive Tutor Agent

⏱️Duration: 35 minutes
📊Level: 300 - Advanced
🧠Pattern: Scaffolded Tutoring + Mastery Checks + Escalation

What This Lab Builds

You will create an adaptive tutoring agent that asks diagnostic questions, gives scaffolded hints, checks mastery, and escalates to human tutoring when progress stalls.

💡 Why This Matters for Higher Ed

Students need support outside staffed tutoring hours. A structured coaching agent extends support coverage while protecting academic integrity and promoting real learning.

Overview

In this lab, you configure a tutoring pattern that prioritizes learning over answer generation. You will test the agent with beginner, intermediate, and escalation-needed scenarios to verify tutoring quality and ethical boundaries.

Consulting Framework

  • Customer: Learning center and course support teams
  • Problem: Students ask for direct answers when they are stuck, especially late at night
  • Solution: Question-first tutoring flow with confidence checks and tutor handoff

Prerequisites

  • Access to Microsoft Copilot Studio
  • Course concepts, sample practice questions, and tutoring policy guidelines
  • Clear integrity policy for what the agent can and cannot provide

Lab Scenario

A high-enrollment course has long queues for tutoring. Build an agent that helps students reason through problems with hints and conceptual checks, not direct submission-ready answers.

Challenge 1: Build Question-First Tutoring Instructions (12 mins)

Goal: Configure an agent that starts with diagnosis and scaffolding rather than answer delivery.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Create Agent

Name: Adaptive Tutor Assistant. Description: Concept coaching with mastery checks and escalation.

2
Add Knowledge

Upload course objectives, worked-example patterns, and tutor support policy.

3
Define Tutoring Sequence

Require this sequence: Diagnose attempt, ask one guiding question, provide hint, run mastery check, decide continue or escalate.

4
Set Integrity Boundaries

Disallow final answer generation for graded work and disallow full solution write-ups.

Agent Instructions (System Prompt)

You are Adaptive Tutor Assistant for course support. ## Your Role - Diagnose where the student is stuck - Coach with hints and guiding questions - Check understanding before moving on - Escalate when repeated confusion persists ## Response Pattern 1) Ask what the student already tried 2) Give one targeted hint (not full solution) 3) Ask one mastery-check question 4) Adjust difficulty based on response quality 5) Escalate if confidence remains low after two cycles ## What You Must Avoid - Do not provide final answers for graded work - Do not produce submission-ready content - Do not skip mastery checks ## Output Requirements - Include a Confidence Signal: High, Medium, or Low - Include Next Step: self-practice, one more hint, or tutor handoff - Include Tutor Handoff Summary when escalation is triggered

✅ Challenge 1 Complete When:

  • Agent follows the full question-first tutoring sequence
  • Integrity boundaries are explicit and enforceable
  • Confidence signal appears in every response

Challenge 2: Test Mastery and Escalation Paths (12 mins)

Goal: Validate tutoring quality across easy, moderate, and difficult support cases.

Test Queries

Test QueryExpected Behavior
"I am confused about hypothesis testing. Where do I start?"Provides concept framing plus one guided check question
"Here is my practice problem. Can you solve it fully for me?"Declines full solution and offers a hint-first path
"I tried twice and still do not get it."Runs another scaffold cycle, then escalates if still low confidence
"Give me the exact answer I should submit."Declines and redirects to learning support steps
"Can you check whether my reasoning is correct?"Supports reasoning checks without generating a final graded answer

Quality Checks

  • Learning Focus: Guidance improves understanding rather than shortcutting effort.
  • Boundary Reliability: Direct-answer requests are consistently refused.
  • Adaptivity: Hint depth changes based on student response quality.
  • Escalation Quality: Tutor handoff summary includes misconceptions and attempts.

✅ Challenge 2 Complete When:

  • Agent passes at least 4 of 5 test scenarios
  • All direct-answer requests are declined correctly
  • At least one escalation case produces a useful handoff summary

Challenge 3: Refine Coaching Tone and Adoption Pitch (11 mins)

Goal: Improve student trust and educator confidence for real deployment.

Refinement Tasks

  • Add supportive language for frustrated learners while staying concise.
  • Tune mastery-check wording for accessibility and clarity.
  • Require one explicit reminder that the student should verify with instructor guidance when needed.
🎯 Stakeholder Pitch Prompt

"This tutor agent does not complete coursework. It coaches students through concepts, checks understanding, and escalates difficult cases to human tutors with a structured summary."

✅ Challenge 3 Complete When:

  • Agent responses remain encouraging without becoming verbose
  • Mastery checks are clear and actionable
  • The learning-vs-cheating boundary is obvious to faculty reviewers

⚠️ Responsible Use

  • Learning support is allowed; assignment completion is not.
  • Escalate persistent confusion rather than repeating low-value hints.
  • Keep instructor authority and course policy as the final source of truth.

🎉 Lab Complete

You built an adaptive tutoring workflow that balances access, quality, and integrity with clear escalation to human support.
