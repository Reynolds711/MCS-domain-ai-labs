Lab 301-1: Academic Advising Agent

🎓 Lab 301-1: Build an Academic Advising Agent

⏱️Duration: 35 minutes
📊Level: 300 - Advanced
🧠Pattern: Multi-Turn + Policy Grounding + Handoff

What This Lab Builds

You will build a multi-turn advising agent that collects student context, identifies degree requirement gaps, and produces an advisor-ready handoff summary instead of generic recommendations.

💡 Why This Matters for Higher Ed

Advising offices face recurring planning questions. A strong intake and routing agent reduces repetitive back-and-forth, shortens appointment prep time, and gives advisors more time for complex student needs.

Overview

In this lab, you will configure an advising agent in Copilot Studio, ground it on degree maps and advising policies, test it against common and edge-case student scenarios, and tune escalation logic for high-risk pathways.

Consulting Framework

  • Customer: Academic advising team for first- and second-year undergraduates
  • Problem: Advisors spend 15 to 20 minutes per case collecting baseline context before they can deliver guidance
  • Solution: Agent-led intake, degree-gap summary, and structured advisor handoff

Prerequisites

  • Access to Microsoft Copilot Studio
  • Sample advising inputs: degree map, catalog rules, prerequisite chart, registration timeline
  • Understanding of one target major (for realistic testing)

Lab Scenario

The advising office is overloaded before registration. Students ask similar questions about required courses, sequence, and graduation timing. The office wants an agent that does early triage and hands advisors a clean summary with known risks.

Challenge 1: Configure Agent and Intake Flow (12 mins)

Goal: Build a multi-turn intake that captures enough context to produce useful, reviewable recommendations.

Step-by-Step Instructions

1
Create Agent

Name: Academic Advising Assistant. Description: Intake and planning support for degree progression.

2
Add Knowledge Sources

Upload degree checklist, catalog prerequisites, transfer policy, and registration dates.

3
Build Intake Questions

Collect major, completed credits, transfer credits, target graduation term, work schedule constraints, and preferred modality.

4
Define Output Contract

Require sections: Student Goal, Completed vs Remaining Requirements, Recommended Next-Term Courses, Risk Flags, Advisor Follow-Up Questions.

Agent Instructions (System Prompt)

You are Academic Advising Assistant for undergraduate planning support. ## Your Role - Collect planning context before giving guidance - Compare student record details against policy-grounded requirements - Recommend next-term options with rationale and risk notes - Produce advisor-ready handoff summaries ## Response Rules - Ask only essential follow-up questions - Separate confirmed facts from assumptions - Cite which policy or planning artifact informed your recommendation - Use clear, student-friendly language ## Required Output Format 1) Student Goal 2) Requirement Status (Complete, In Progress, Remaining) 3) Recommended Next-Term Courses with rationale 4) Risk Flags and timeline concerns 5) Advisor Follow-Up Questions ## Boundaries - Do not finalize registration decisions - Do not overrule degree policy or advisor judgment - Escalate unclear transfer-credit mapping and probation-risk scenarios

✅ Challenge 1 Complete When:

  • Agent captures all required intake fields in a structured flow
  • Recommendations are returned in the exact output format
  • At least one policy source is referenced in responses

Challenge 2: Evaluate Degree-Gap and Schedule Quality (12 mins)

Goal: Validate recommendation quality across common and complex advising scenarios.

Test Queries

ScenarioExpected Behavior
First-year student missing math prerequisiteRecommends prerequisite path and explains sequencing impact
Transfer student with ambiguous equivalent creditFlags ambiguity and escalates for advisor review
Student working 30 hrs/week asks for 18 creditsSuggests load adjustment and risk note with rationale
Student near graduation with one missing capstone requirementHighlights capstone dependency and urgency timeline
Student asks "just tell me what to register for" with missing contextRequests key intake details before recommending

Quality Checks

  • Accuracy: Requirement mapping matches provided policy docs.
  • Actionability: Next steps include owner and timing.
  • Risk Awareness: Graduation delay risks are clearly surfaced.
  • Escalation: Ambiguous cases route to human advisors.

✅ Challenge 2 Complete When:

  • At least 4 of 5 scenarios return policy-aligned recommendations
  • All ambiguous cases contain explicit escalation language
  • Every response includes a clear next action for the student

Challenge 3: Refine Escalation and Handoff Summary (11 mins)

Goal: Improve trust by ensuring advisors receive concise, high-signal case summaries.

Refinement Tasks

  • Add escalation triggers for probation risk, unresolved transfer credit, and prerequisite conflicts.
  • Require a one-paragraph advisor handoff summary with student goal, blockers, and decision points.
  • Add confidence labeling: High, Medium, or Needs Human Review.
🎯 Stakeholder Pitch Prompt

"This advising agent handles structured intake and policy-grounded first-pass planning. It does not replace advisors. It reduces repetitive intake time so advisors can focus on nuanced student decisions."

✅ Challenge 3 Complete When:

  • Advisor handoff summary is readable in under 60 seconds
  • Escalation triggers fire consistently in edge cases
  • Confidence labels align with case ambiguity

⚠️ Responsible Use

  • The agent supports planning; advisors approve final academic decisions.
  • Do not infer sensitive student status not present in provided data.
  • Surface policy uncertainty instead of guessing.

🎉 Lab Complete

You built an advising workflow agent with policy grounding, quality validation, and practical handoff support for real advising operations.
