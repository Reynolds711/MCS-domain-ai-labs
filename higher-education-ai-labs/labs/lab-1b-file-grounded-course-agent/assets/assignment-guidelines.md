# Major Assignment 1: Business Proposal (25%)

## Overview

The Business Proposal is the signature assignment for this course. You will identify a real business problem and propose a solution, demonstrating your ability to analyze audiences, construct persuasive arguments, and present information professionally.

## Assignment Description

You will write a formal business proposal (2,500-3,000 words) that addresses a genuine business communication challenge. Your proposal should be directed to a specific decision-maker (real or hypothetical) who has the authority to implement your recommendations.

## Topic Selection

Choose ONE of the following approaches:

**Option A: Organizational Problem**
- Identify a communication problem at an organization where you work, volunteer, or have connections
- Examples: Ineffective meeting practices, poor interdepartmental communication, customer service communication gaps

**Option B: Case-Based Problem**
- Select from the provided case studies (posted on Canvas)
- Analyze the communication breakdown and propose solutions

**Option C: Approved Original Topic**
- Propose your own topic to Professor Kim by Week 4
- Must involve a clear business communication challenge

## Required Sections

Your proposal must include the following sections:

### 1. Executive Summary (250-300 words)
- Brief overview of the problem, proposed solution, and key benefits
- Should stand alone as a summary of the entire document
- Write this LAST, even though it appears first

### 2. Introduction (200-250 words)
- Context and background of the organization
- Clear statement of the problem
- Purpose of the proposal
- Preview of what's to come

### 3. Problem Analysis (500-600 words)
- Detailed description of the current situation
- Evidence that the problem exists (data, examples, research)
- Impact of the problem on the organization
- Root causes identified

### 4. Proposed Solution (600-700 words)
- Clear, specific recommendations
- How the solution addresses the root causes
- Step-by-step implementation plan
- Timeline for implementation

### 5. Benefits and Justification (400-500 words)
- Expected outcomes and benefits
- Cost-benefit analysis (if applicable)
- How success will be measured
- Comparison to alternatives (why this solution?)

### 6. Budget and Resources (200-300 words)
- Estimated costs
- Required personnel and time
- Other resources needed

### 7. Conclusion (150-200 words)
- Summary of key points
- Call to action
- Restatement of main benefit

### 8. References
- APA format (7th edition)
- Minimum 5 credible sources
- At least 2 must be academic/peer-reviewed

## Format Requirements

| Element | Requirement |
|---------|-------------|
| Length | 2,500-3,000 words (excluding references) |
| Font | 12pt Times New Roman or 11pt Calibri |
| Spacing | Single-spaced with double space between sections |
| Margins | 1 inch all sides |
| Headings | Use clear, professional headings for each section |
| Page Numbers | Bottom right, starting on page 2 |
| File Format | PDF or .docx |
| File Naming | LastName_FirstName_BusinessProposal.pdf |

## Submission Requirements

- **Draft:** Submit via Canvas by February 20, 2026, 11:59 PM
- **Final:** Submit via Canvas by March 6, 2026, 11:59 PM
- Both the draft and final must be submitted—draft submission is required to receive feedback

## Evaluation Criteria

See the Business Proposal Rubric for detailed grading criteria. Key areas:

1. Problem identification and analysis (25%)
2. Solution quality and feasibility (25%)
3. Persuasive argumentation (20%)
4. Professional writing and formatting (20%)
5. Research and sources (10%)

# Major Assignment 2: Research Report (15%)

## Overview

The Research Report demonstrates your ability to gather, synthesize, and present business communication research in a clear, professional format.

## Assignment Description

You will write a research report (1,500-2,000 words) investigating a current trend or issue in business communication. Your report should inform readers about the topic and provide actionable recommendations.

## Topic Options

Select ONE topic from the following categories:

**Digital Communication**
- Virtual team communication challenges and solutions
- Email overload and productivity
- Social media policy in organizations
- Video conferencing fatigue and best practices

**Organizational Communication**
- Remote work communication strategies
- Generational differences in workplace communication
- Internal communication during change management
- Feedback cultures in modern organizations

**Professional Development**
- Soft skills most valued by employers
- Personal branding and professional communication
- Cross-cultural communication competence
- AI's impact on business communication

**Other:** Propose your own topic (requires approval by Week 8)

## Required Sections

### 1. Executive Summary (150-200 words)
- Overview of the research topic, key findings, and recommendations

### 2. Introduction (200-250 words)
- Background on the topic
- Why this topic matters for business communication
- Research questions guiding the report

### 3. Literature Review (600-800 words)
- Synthesis of existing research
- Key themes and findings from sources
- Gaps in current knowledge

### 4. Findings and Analysis (400-500 words)
- Your analysis of the research
- Patterns, trends, or insights discovered
- Connection to business communication theory

### 5. Recommendations (300-400 words)
- 3-5 specific, actionable recommendations
- Based on research findings
- Applicable to professional settings

### 6. Conclusion (100-150 words)
- Summary of key insights
- Future directions or unanswered questions

### 7. References
- APA format (7th edition)
- Minimum 8 sources
- At least 4 must be peer-reviewed academic sources

# Major Assignment 3: Professional Presentation (20%)

## Overview

The Professional Presentation allows you to demonstrate oral communication competence by presenting your business proposal or research findings to the class.

## Assignment Description

You will deliver a 10-12 minute presentation to the class, showcasing either:
- Your Business Proposal (recommended)
- Your Research Report findings

You will also participate in a 3-5 minute Q&A session following your presentation.

## Presentation Requirements

### Content Requirements
1. **Opening Hook** (30-60 seconds)
   - Attention-grabbing start
   - Establish credibility and relevance

2. **Clear Structure**
   - Preview of main points
   - Logical flow
   - Transitions between sections

3. **Visual Aids**
   - 8-12 slides maximum
   - Professional design
   - Minimal text (no "wall of words")
   - Relevant visuals and data displays

4. **Evidence and Examples**
   - Support claims with data
   - Use specific examples
   - Cite sources verbally

5. **Conclusion**
   - Clear summary
   - Call to action or takeaway
   - Memorable closing

### Delivery Requirements
- Maintain eye contact with audience
- Speak clearly at appropriate pace
- Use natural gestures
- Move purposefully (don't stand rigidly)
- Stay within time limits

### Time Limits
- **Presentation:** 10-12 minutes
- **Q&A:** 3-5 minutes
- Going under 9 minutes or over 14 minutes will result in point deductions

## Slide Design Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use consistent color scheme
- Include slide numbers
- Use high-quality images
- Limit bullet points to 4-5 per slide
- Use large, readable fonts (24pt minimum)

**Don't:**
- Read directly from slides
- Include full sentences as bullet points
- Use distracting transitions or animations
- Overcrowd slides with information
- Use low-resolution images

## Presentation Schedule

Presentations will occur during Weeks 14:
- **April 22 (Tuesday):** Presenters 1-12
- **April 24 (Thursday):** Presenters 13-25

Presentation order determined by random draw during Week 13. You cannot choose your presentation date.

## Submission Requirements

1. **Slides:** Submit via Canvas by 11:59 PM the night BEFORE your presentation
2. **Self-Reflection:** Submit 1-page reflection within 48 hours after presenting
   - What went well?
   - What would you do differently?
   - Key lessons learned?

## Evaluation Criteria

See the Professional Presentation Rubric for details. Key areas:
- Content and organization (30%)
- Delivery and presence (30%)
- Visual aids (20%)
- Q&A handling (10%)
- Time management (10%)
