# COMM 301: Business Communication
## Grading Rubrics

---

# Business Proposal Rubric (250 points)

## Scoring Scale
- **Excellent (90-100%):** Exceeds expectations; demonstrates mastery
- **Good (80-89%):** Meets expectations; demonstrates competence
- **Satisfactory (70-79%):** Partially meets expectations; adequate but has gaps
- **Needs Improvement (60-69%):** Below expectations; significant weaknesses
- **Unsatisfactory (Below 60%):** Does not meet minimum requirements

---

## Criterion 1: Problem Identification and Analysis (62.5 points - 25%)

### Excellent (56-62.5 points)
- Problem is clearly defined, specific, and significant
- Thorough analysis using multiple forms of evidence
- Root causes identified with clear reasoning
- Demonstrates deep understanding of organizational context
- Impact on organization convincingly documented

### Good (50-55.5 points)
- Problem is well-defined and relevant
- Solid analysis with supporting evidence
- Root causes identified
- Good understanding of context
- Impact adequately documented

### Satisfactory (44-49.5 points)
- Problem is identifiable but could be more specific
- Some analysis present but lacking depth
- Root causes mentioned but not fully developed
- Basic understanding of context
- Impact mentioned but not fully explored

### Needs Improvement (37.5-43.5 points)
- Problem is vague or too broad
- Minimal analysis or evidence
- Root causes unclear
- Limited understanding of context
- Impact not clearly established

### Unsatisfactory (Below 37.5 points)
- Problem not clearly identified
- No meaningful analysis
- Missing or irrelevant evidence
- No understanding of context demonstrated

---

## Criterion 2: Proposed Solution Quality (62.5 points - 25%)

### Excellent (56-62.5 points)
- Solution is innovative, specific, and clearly addresses root causes
- Implementation plan is detailed with realistic timeline
- Feasibility thoroughly considered
- Potential obstacles anticipated with mitigation strategies
- Solution clearly connects to problem analysis

### Good (50-55.5 points)
- Solution is appropriate and addresses main issues
- Implementation plan is clear and reasonable
- Feasibility addressed
- Some consideration of obstacles
- Clear connection to problem

### Satisfactory (44-49.5 points)
- Solution is reasonable but lacks specificity
- Implementation plan is present but vague
- Limited discussion of feasibility
- Obstacles not fully considered
- Connection to problem could be stronger

### Needs Improvement (37.5-43.5 points)
- Solution is generic or impractical
- Implementation plan unclear or unrealistic
- Feasibility not considered
- No discussion of obstacles
- Weak connection to problem

### Unsatisfactory (Below 37.5 points)
- Solution missing or inappropriate
- No implementation plan
- Clearly infeasible
- Does not address the stated problem

---

## Criterion 3: Persuasive Argumentation (50 points - 20%)

### Excellent (45-50 points)
- Arguments are compelling and logically structured
- Claims supported with strong evidence and reasoning
- Counterarguments anticipated and addressed
- Audience needs clearly understood and addressed
- Clear call to action

### Good (40-44.5 points)
- Arguments are logical and well-supported
- Most claims have evidence
- Some consideration of counterarguments
- Audience awareness evident
- Call to action present

### Satisfactory (35-39.5 points)
- Arguments present but could be stronger
- Some claims unsupported
- Limited consideration of counterarguments
- Basic audience awareness
- Weak call to action

### Needs Improvement (30-34.5 points)
- Arguments weak or illogical
- Many claims unsupported
- No counterargument consideration
- Poor audience awareness
- No clear call to action

### Unsatisfactory (Below 30 points)
- No clear argumentative structure
- Claims without support
- Logic errors throughout

---

## Criterion 4: Professional Writing and Formatting (50 points - 20%)

### Excellent (45-50 points)
- Writing is clear, concise, and engaging
- Professional tone maintained throughout
- Error-free grammar, spelling, punctuation
- Formatting follows all guidelines precisely
- Headings, visuals, and layout enhance readability

### Good (40-44.5 points)
- Writing is clear and professional
- Tone appropriate
- Few minor errors
- Formatting follows guidelines
- Good use of headings and layout

### Satisfactory (35-39.5 points)
- Writing is understandable but could be clearer
- Tone inconsistent at times
- Several errors present
- Most formatting guidelines followed
- Basic organization

### Needs Improvement (30-34.5 points)
- Writing unclear or unprofessional
- Tone inappropriate for business context
- Many errors that distract reader
- Formatting guidelines not followed
- Poor organization

### Unsatisfactory (Below 30 points)
- Writing difficult to understand
- Unprofessional throughout
- Errors impede comprehension
- Formatting ignored

---

## Criterion 5: Research and Sources (25 points - 10%)

### Excellent (22.5-25 points)
- 6+ high-quality, relevant sources
- Sources effectively integrated
- Perfect APA formatting
- Strong mix of academic and industry sources
- Sources directly support claims

### Good (20-22 points)
- 5 quality sources
- Sources well-integrated
- APA formatting with minor errors
- Good source variety
- Sources support main points

### Satisfactory (17.5-19.5 points)
- 5 sources, varying quality
- Sources present but not always integrated
- Some APA errors
- Limited source variety
- Some irrelevant sources

### Needs Improvement (15-17 points)
- Fewer than 5 sources
- Sources poorly integrated
- Significant APA errors
- Sources not credible or relevant

### Unsatisfactory (Below 15 points)
- Missing or inadequate sources
- No integration
- No APA formatting
- Unreliable sources

---

# Professional Presentation Rubric (200 points)

## Criterion 1: Content and Organization (60 points - 30%)

### Excellent (54-60 points)
- Opening immediately engages audience
- Content is substantive, accurate, and relevant
- Clear, logical structure with smooth transitions
- Key points emphasized effectively
- Strong, memorable conclusion
- All claims supported with evidence

### Good (48-53.5 points)
- Effective opening
- Content is solid and relevant
- Clear structure
- Good transitions
- Clear conclusion
- Most claims supported

### Satisfactory (42-47.5 points)
- Opening present but not engaging
- Content adequate but could go deeper
- Structure evident but transitions choppy
- Conclusion present but weak
- Some unsupported claims

### Needs Improvement (36-41.5 points)
- Weak opening
- Content superficial or disorganized
- Structure unclear
- Abrupt or missing conclusion
- Many unsupported claims

### Unsatisfactory (Below 36 points)
- No clear opening
- Content inaccurate or irrelevant
- No discernible structure
- No conclusion

---

## Criterion 2: Delivery and Presence (60 points - 30%)

### Excellent (54-60 points)
- Confident, natural delivery
- Excellent eye contact with entire audience
- Voice clear, varied, and appropriately paced
- Natural gestures and movement
- Engages audience throughout
- Handles nervousness effectively

### Good (48-53.5 points)
- Solid delivery
- Good eye contact
- Voice clear and audible
- Appropriate gestures
- Generally engaging
- Mostly comfortable

### Satisfactory (42-47.5 points)
- Adequate delivery
- Some eye contact
- Voice sometimes unclear or monotone
- Limited gestures
- Occasionally engaging
- Some visible nervousness

### Needs Improvement (36-41.5 points)
- Weak delivery
- Minimal eye contact
- Voice difficult to hear or very monotone
- No meaningful gestures
- Not engaging
- Nervousness interferes with presentation

### Unsatisfactory (Below 36 points)
- Unable to deliver effectively
- No eye contact (reading notes/slides)
- Cannot be heard or understood
- Distracting behaviors

---

## Criterion 3: Visual Aids (40 points - 20%)

### Excellent (36-40 points)
- Slides professionally designed and visually appealing
- Perfect balance of text and visuals
- Data visualizations clear and effective
- Slides enhance (not replace) the speaker
- Consistent, appropriate design throughout
- No errors

### Good (32-35.5 points)
- Slides well-designed
- Good balance of elements
- Data displays clear
- Slides support speaker
- Consistent design
- Minor errors only

### Satisfactory (28-31.5 points)
- Slides adequate
- Some text-heavy slides
- Basic visuals
- Slides sometimes distract
- Some inconsistency
- Some errors

### Needs Improvement (24-27.5 points)
- Slides poorly designed
- Too much text
- Ineffective or missing visuals
- Slides distract from message
- Inconsistent or unprofessional
- Multiple errors

### Unsatisfactory (Below 24 points)
- No slides or slides unreadable
- All text, no visuals
- Slides detract from presentation

---

## Criterion 4: Q&A Handling (20 points - 10%)

### Excellent (18-20 points)
- Listens carefully to questions
- Responds thoughtfully and thoroughly
- Handles difficult questions with poise
- Admits when doesn't know something
- Engages questioners respectfully

### Good (16-17.5 points)
- Listens to questions
- Provides adequate responses
- Handles most questions well
- Generally honest about limitations
- Respectful engagement

### Satisfactory (14-15.5 points)
- Sometimes doesn't fully understand questions
- Responses adequate but brief
- Struggles with challenging questions
- Some evasiveness
- Generally respectful

### Needs Improvement (12-13.5 points)
- Doesn't listen carefully
- Responses inadequate
- Cannot handle questions
- Defensive or dismissive
- Unprofessional demeanor

### Unsatisfactory (Below 12 points)
- Does not engage with Q&A
- Cannot answer any questions
- Rude or inappropriate behavior

---

## Criterion 5: Time Management (20 points - 10%)

### Excellent (18-20 points)
- Presentation length 10-12 minutes exactly
- Pacing appropriate throughout
- Time for all sections as planned
- Smooth conclusion within time

### Good (16-17.5 points)
- Presentation 9-13 minutes
- Generally good pacing
- All sections covered
- Minor rushing at end

### Satisfactory (14-15.5 points)
- Presentation 8-14 minutes
- Some pacing issues
- Some sections rushed or cut
- Noticeable time management issues

### Needs Improvement (12-13.5 points)
- Presentation under 8 or over 14 minutes
- Poor pacing
- Major sections missing or rushed
- Significant time management problems

### Unsatisfactory (Below 12 points)
- Presentation under 6 or over 16 minutes
- Pacing makes presentation difficult to follow

# Research Report Rubric (150 points)

## Criterion 1: Research Quality (45 points - 30%)

### Excellent (40.5-45 points)
- 8+ high-quality sources with strong academic representation
- Literature thoroughly synthesized, not just summarized
- Clear themes identified across sources
- Research directly relevant to topic
- Gaps in knowledge identified

### Good (36-40 points)
- 8 quality sources
- Good synthesis
- Themes present
- Sources relevant
- Some gap identification

### Satisfactory (31.5-35.5 points)
- Minimum sources met
- More summary than synthesis
- Limited theme development
- Most sources relevant
- Limited analysis

### Needs Improvement (27-31 points)
- Fewer than 8 sources
- No synthesis
- Sources listed, not analyzed
- Some irrelevant sources

### Unsatisfactory (Below 27 points)
- Inadequate sources
- No meaningful engagement with research

---

- Some practical application

- Weak research connection

### Needs Improvement (27-31 points)

### Unsatisfactory (Below 27 points)

# Case Analysis Rubric (50 points each)

## Problem Identification (15 points)
- **Excellent (13.5-15):** Problem clearly identified with nuance
- **Good (12-13):** Problem well-identified
- **Satisfactory (10.5-11.5):** Problem identified but surface-level
- **Needs Improvement (9-10):** Problem vague
- **Unsatisfactory (Below 9):** Problem not identified

## Application of Course Concepts (20 points)
- **Excellent (18-20):** Multiple concepts applied insightfully
- **Good (16-17.5):** Concepts applied appropriately
- **Satisfactory (14-15.5):** Limited concept application
- **Needs Improvement (12-13.5):** Weak concept application
- **Unsatisfactory (Below 12):** No concepts applied

## Recommendations (15 points)
- **Excellent (13.5-15):** Specific, actionable, well-reasoned
- **Good (12-13):** Clear, reasonable recommendations
- **Satisfactory (10.5-11.5):** Generic recommendations
- **Needs Improvement (9-10):** Vague or impractical
- **Unsatisfactory (Below 9):** Missing or irrelevant

# Participation Rubric (100 points)

## In-Class Engagement (50 points)
- **Excellent:** Consistently contributes thoughtful comments, asks questions, engages with peers
- **Good:** Regularly participates with relevant contributions
- **Satisfactory:** Occasional participation, mostly attentive
- **Needs Improvement:** Rarely participates, often disengaged
- **Unsatisfactory:** Does not participate, frequently absent or distracted

## Professional Behavior (50 points)
- **Excellent:** Always prepared, respectful, on time, technology used appropriately
- **Good:** Usually prepared, respectful, minimal tardiness
- **Satisfactory:** Sometimes unprepared, occasional issues
- **Needs Improvement:** Often unprepared, disrespectful behaviors
- **Unsatisfactory:** Chronic issues with professionalism
