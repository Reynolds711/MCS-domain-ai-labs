## Course Description

This course provides a comprehensive introduction to business communication theory and practice. Students will develop competencies in written, oral, and digital communication within professional contexts. Emphasis is placed on audience analysis, message construction, professional document design, and effective presentation delivery. Students will produce business documents including memos, reports, proposals, and presentations while developing critical thinking skills for communication challenges in diverse organizational settings.

---

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this course, students will be able to:

1. **Analyze audiences** to tailor messages for maximum effectiveness in professional contexts
2. **Compose professional documents** including business memos, reports, proposals, and correspondence
3. **Deliver effective presentations** using appropriate visual aids and professional speaking techniques
4. **Apply principles of persuasion** in business writing and speaking situations
5. **Demonstrate proficiency** in digital communication tools and professional email etiquette
6. **Evaluate and critique** business communication for clarity, conciseness, and impact
7. **Work collaboratively** in teams to produce professional-quality deliverables
8. **Navigate intercultural communication** challenges in globalized business environments

## Course Policies

### Attendance Policy

Attendance is essential for success in this course. Students are expected to attend all class sessions.

- **Excused absences:** Illness, family emergencies, university-sanctioned activities (documentation required)
- **Unexcused absences:** 
  - 1-2: No penalty
  - 3-4: Final grade reduced by one letter grade step (e.g., B+ to B)
  - 5+: Final grade reduced by full letter grade (e.g., B to C)
- **Tardiness:** Three tardies (more than 5 minutes late) = one unexcused absence

To request an excused absence, email Professor Kim **before** the class session when possible.

### Late Submission Policy

- Assignments submitted late will receive a **10% deduction per day** (including weekends)
- Maximum deduction: 50% (assignments more than 5 days late receive maximum 50% credit)
- **No late submissions accepted after 7 days** without prior approval
- Discussion posts cannot be submitted late

**Exceptions:** Extensions may be granted for documented emergencies. Contact Professor Kim **before** the deadline when possible.

### Academic Integrity

All work submitted must be your own. Violations include:
- Plagiarism (using others' words or ideas without citation)
- Unauthorized collaboration
- Submitting work from other courses without permission
- Using AI tools (like ChatGPT) to generate submitted content without disclosure

**AI Policy:** You may use AI tools for brainstorming, grammar checking, and research. You may NOT submit AI-generated text as your own writing. When AI assists your work, you must disclose this in an "AI Acknowledgment" statement.

**Consequences:** First violation: Zero on assignment + meeting with instructor. Second violation: Course failure + referral to Dean of Students.

### Communication Policy

- Check your university email **daily**—this is the official communication channel
- Include "COMM 301" in email subject lines
- Allow 24 hours for email responses (48 hours on weekends)
- For urgent matters, attend office hours or call the department office

### Technology Policy

- Laptops/tablets permitted for class activities only
- Phones should be silenced and put away
- Recording requires advance permission
- Virtual backgrounds required for any video calls
