SMBs win by moving quickly with limited resources. These labs show how to build practical, low-code agents in Microsoft Copilot Studio that reduce repetitive work, improve response times, and help teams focus on revenue-generating and customer-facing priorities.

Why Agentic AI for SMB?

  • Automate high-volume tasks: Reduce repetitive admin work without adding headcount

⚠️ Responsible AI Guidelines for SMB

All labs emphasize safe and practical deployment:

  • Assist, don't over-automate: Keep human approval for high-impact decisions
  • Ground in trusted sources: Use approved internal content and clear source boundaries
  • Protect sensitive data: Exclude confidential customer and financial data from public demos
  • Measure outcomes: Track quality, cycle time, and business impact continuously
