Lab 1A: Web-Grounded Growth Intelligence Agent

💼 Lab 1A: Build a Web-Grounded SMB Growth Intelligence Agent

⏱️Duration: 30 minutes
📊Level: 100 - Beginner
🌐Grounding: Web Sources

Overview

In this lab, you will build a declarative agent that gathers and summarizes market signals for SMB leadership. The agent helps teams move from scattered research to concise, action-ready insights.

Consulting Framework

  • Customer: SMB owner and sales/marketing lead
  • Problem: Manual market research is inconsistent and time-consuming
  • Solution: Web-grounded agent that delivers structured competitive insights

Challenge 1: Build the Agent (10 mins)

  1. Open Microsoft Copilot Studio and create a new agent.
  2. Name it SMB Growth Intelligence Agent.
  3. Add these instruction goals: summarize competitor positioning, pricing signals, and trend changes.
  4. Add public website knowledge sources from target competitor pages and one industry publication.

✅ Complete When

  • Agent is created with a clear business role
  • At least 2 web sources are connected
  • Instruction set includes concise summary format

Challenge 2: Test with Business Prompts (10 mins)

Run these prompts in the test panel:

  • "Summarize 3 changes in competitor messaging this month."
  • "What pricing signals suggest a promotion or packaging change?"
  • "Give me a one-page executive brief with risks and opportunities."
Tip: Require output sections: Signal, Evidence, Why it matters, Suggested action.

Challenge 3: Add Guardrails and ROI (10 mins)

  • Require source-backed statements and uncertainty callouts.
  • Forbid financial or legal recommendations.
  • Define an ROI baseline: weekly research hours before/after.

Reflection

How would this agent change your weekly leadership meeting prep and decision speed?

Deliverable

Produce a sample "Monday Growth Brief" with 3 market signals, 2 risks, and 2 recommended next actions.
