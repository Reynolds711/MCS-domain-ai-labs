# SMB SOP Playbook

## Customer Onboarding
1. Confirm signed service agreement.
2. Create account and assign owner.
3. Schedule kickoff call within 2 business days.
4. Send onboarding checklist and timeline.

## Quote Approval
- Standard quotes under $5,000: sales lead approval.
- Quotes $5,000-$20,000: operations manager approval.
- Quotes above $20,000: owner approval required.

## Incident Handling
- Severity 1 (service outage): immediate escalation to on-call lead.
- Severity 2 (major degradation): response target within 2 hours.
- Severity 3 (minor issue): response target within 1 business day.
