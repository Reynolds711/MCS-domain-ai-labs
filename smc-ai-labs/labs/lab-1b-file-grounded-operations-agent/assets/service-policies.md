# Service Policies

## Refund Policy
- Full refund within 14 days if implementation has not started.
- Partial refund (up to 50%) within 30 days, based on work completed.
- No standard refunds after 30 days; escalate exceptional cases.

## Response SLA
- Critical issues: first response within 1 hour.
- High priority: first response within 4 hours.
- Normal issues: first response within 1 business day.

## Escalation Rules
Escalate to the operations manager when:
- Customer requests exception to refund policy.
- Discount request exceeds approved threshold.
- Issue has legal, compliance, or contractual implications.
