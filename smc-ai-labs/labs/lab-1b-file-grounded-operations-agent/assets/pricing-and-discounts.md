# Pricing and Discount Policy

## Standard Discount Bands
- 0-5%: account manager discretion.
- 6-10%: sales lead approval.
- 11-15%: operations manager approval.
- >15%: owner approval and written business case.

## Renewal Discounts
- Returning customer in good standing: up to 8% with sales lead approval.
- Multi-year contract: up to 12% with operations manager approval.

## Non-Discount Alternatives
- Add onboarding services at no charge (max 3 hours).
- Offer extended payment terms for qualified accounts.
