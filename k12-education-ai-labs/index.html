⚠️ Ethical Guidelines for K-12 AI Agents

All labs emphasize responsible AI practices:

  • Augment, don't replace: Agents assist educators, never make decisions
  • Transparency: Always disclose when AI is being used
  • Privacy first: Never use real student data in labs
  • Human oversight: Teachers review all AI-generated content
  • Equity focus: Ensure agents serve all students and families
