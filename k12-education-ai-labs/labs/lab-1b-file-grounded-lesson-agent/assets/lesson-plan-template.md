## SECTION 2: LEARNING OBJECTIVES

### Requirements
Every lesson must include 1-3 measurable learning objectives that students will achieve by the end of the lesson.

### Objective Format
Use the format: "Students will be able to [VERB] + [WHAT] + [CONDITION/CONTEXT]"

### Strong Action Verbs by Bloom's Level
- **Remember:** Define, identify, list, name, recall, recognize
- **Understand:** Describe, explain, summarize, paraphrase, classify
- **Apply:** Demonstrate, use, solve, implement, execute
- **Analyze:** Compare, contrast, examine, differentiate, organize
- **Evaluate:** Assess, critique, judge, justify, defend
- **Create:** Design, construct, develop, compose, formulate

### Example Objectives
- "Students will be able to identify the components of an effective claim in argumentative writing"
- "Students will be able to evaluate evidence for relevance and credibility using a provided rubric"
- "Students will be able to construct a claim-evidence-reasoning paragraph with at least 80% accuracy"

### Avoid These Weak Verbs
- "Understand" (not measurable)
- "Know" (not measurable)
- "Learn about" (not measurable)
- "Be aware of" (not measurable)

## SECTION 5: LESSON PROCEDURES

### Time Management
Include estimated times for each section. Total should equal your lesson duration.

### Required Sections

#### A. Bell Ringer / Do Now (5-10 minutes)
- Brief activity students complete upon entering
- Should activate prior knowledge or preview lesson content
- Must be something students can begin independently

#### B. Introduction / Hook (5-10 minutes)
- Capture student interest
- Connect to prior learning
- State learning objective(s) clearly
- Explain why this matters (relevance)

#### C. Direct Instruction (10-15 minutes)
- Teacher-led explanation of new content
- Include modeling ("I do")
- Check for understanding throughout
- Keep interactive - avoid long lectures

#### D. Guided Practice (10-15 minutes)
- Students practice with teacher support ("We do")
- Circulate and provide feedback
- Address misconceptions immediately
- Gradual release of responsibility

#### E. Independent Practice (10-20 minutes)
- Students apply learning independently ("You do")
- May be started in class, completed as homework
- Clear instructions and expectations
- Built-in accountability

#### F. Closure / Exit Ticket (5-10 minutes)
- Review key learning
- Students demonstrate understanding
- Preview next lesson
- Collect formative assessment data

### Procedure Template
```
Time | Activity | Teacher Actions | Student Actions | Materials
-----|----------|-----------------|-----------------|----------
5 min | Do Now | Monitor, circulate | Complete prompt | Do Now slide
10 min | Hook | Present scenario | Discuss in pairs | Presentation
15 min | Direct Instruction | Model thinking | Take notes, ask questions | Slides, examples
```

## SECTION 6: DIFFERENTIATION

### Requirements
Every lesson must include differentiation strategies for three groups:

#### For Struggling Learners
- Scaffolds, simplified texts, additional support
- Extended time, modified expectations
- Peer support, small group instruction

#### For Advanced Learners
- Extension activities, increased complexity
- Leadership opportunities, independent research
- Application to new contexts

#### For English Language Learners
- Visual supports, vocabulary pre-teaching
- Sentence frames, native language resources
- Reduced linguistic load, peer translation support

### Differentiation Strategies to Consider
| Strategy | Description | Best For |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| Tiered assignments | Same standard, different complexity levels | Mixed-ability classes |
| Flexible grouping | Strategic grouping by need | Targeted support |
| Choice boards | Student choice in demonstrating learning | Student engagement |
| Graphic organizers | Visual structure for thinking | ELL, struggling learners |
| Pre-teaching vocabulary | Front-load key terms | ELL, content-heavy lessons |
| Modified texts | Same content, adjusted reading level | Struggling readers |
| Extension questions | Deeper thinking prompts | Advanced learners |

### Example Differentiation
**For Struggling Learners:**
- Provide partially completed graphic organizer with sentence starters
- Pair with supportive peer for guided practice
- Allow use of example paragraph as reference during independent practice

**For Advanced Learners:**
- Analyze two contrasting arguments and evaluate which is more effective
- Create rubric criteria for evaluating claims (metacognitive extension)
- Write counter-argument paragraph in addition to original claim

**For ELL Students:**
- Pre-teach vocabulary: claim, evidence, reasoning, credible, relevant
- Provide sentence frames: "The author claims that ___. The evidence shows ___."
- Allow bilingual dictionary use during independent practice

### Formative Assessment (During Lesson)

- Quick checks (thumbs up/down, whiteboards, etc.)
- Turn-and-talk monitoring

### Summative Assessment (End of Lesson/Unit)

- Exit ticket
- Quiz/test
- Project/performance task
- Written assignment

### Assessment Alignment
Each learning objective must have a corresponding assessment. Use this format:

| Objective | Assessment | Success Criteria |
|-----------|------------|------------------|
| Identify components of a claim | Exit ticket question 1-2 | 80% accuracy |
| Evaluate evidence for relevance | Guided practice rubric | Score 3+ on rubric |
| Construct CER paragraph | Independent practice | Meets rubric expectations |

## SECTION 8: REFLECTION (Complete After Teaching)

### Questions to Answer
After teaching this lesson, reflect on:

1. **Objective Achievement:** Did students meet the learning objectives? What evidence do you have?

2. **Engagement:** Were students engaged throughout? What worked well? What didn't?

3. **Pacing:** Was timing appropriate? What adjustments would you make?

4. **Differentiation:** Were differentiation strategies effective? What modifications are needed?

5. **Next Steps:** What do students need next? What re-teaching is required?

### Reflection Format
```
What went well:
[Your response]

What I would change:
[Your response]

Student achievement data:
[Percentage who met each objective]

Notes for next time:
[Specific adjustments]
```
