Welcome to State & Local Government AI Labs

Learn to build AI labs aligned to the updated architecture for state and local government. This page follows the source structure directly: Copilot 101 for prompt engineering, Copilot 201 for Studio Lite agents, and Copilot 301 for multi-turn Copilot Studio builds.

Consulting Framework

Each lab answers three critical questions:

  • Who is the customer? Constituents, frontline staff, or internal service teams
  • What repetitive, high-volume problem exists? Clear service, routing, or policy friction
  • What agent can remove friction using existing data? Grounded, practical solutions that improve service delivery
💡 Architecture Alignment

The labs below use the source architecture as the system of record, including updated terminology such as Constituent Experience, Improve Fiscal Stewardship, and Reduce Case Backlogs. Each card uses the same core fields for easier review and demo delivery.

🧠 Copilot 101

Prompt engineering labs focused on simple, 15-minute transformations in Copilot Chat.

101-A: Constituent Communication

⏱️ 15 mins

Persona: City Hall Front Desk Staff

Draft a plain-language email response to a common resident question using clearer structure, tone, and accessible language.

  • Move from a basic permit-answer prompt to a resident-ready response
  • Apply plain language, helpful tone, and explicit next steps
  • Reinforce constituent experience instead of bureaucratic phrasing
Strategic Goal: Improve Constituent Experience
ROI: Faster response drafting, consistent resident communication

101-B: Council Meeting Agenda Builder

⏱️ 15 mins

Persona: City Manager or City Clerk

Turn raw department updates into a structured council meeting agenda with timing, action-item flags, and an executive summary.

  • Transform bullet updates into a governance-ready agenda format
  • Add timing estimates, public-facing language, and action markers
  • Use prompt structure to improve readability for elected officials
Strategic Goal: Improve Fiscal Stewardship
ROI: Agenda prep time cut from 2 hours to 20 minutes

🧭 Copilot 201

Studio Lite labs using one knowledge source and one clear outcome.

Web Grounding

201-A: Civic Information Agent

⏱️ 30 mins

Persona: Constituent

Build a web-grounded agent on public civic websites that answers service and government-procedure questions in plain language.

  • Ground on USA.gov, plainlanguage.gov, and state government sites
  • Return authoritative, easier-to-understand guidance
  • Support constituent experience without staff involvement
Strategic Goal: Improve Constituent Experience
ROI: 24/7 access to civic information without staff involvement
File Grounding

201-B: Policy Compliance Agent

⏱️ 30 mins

Persona: Government Staff

Build a file-grounded agent on a provided policy document so staff can get accurate, policy-grounded answers with citations.

  • Use a shipped policy document rather than external search
  • Support more consistent policy interpretation across departments
  • Focus on accurate, document-grounded answers
Strategic Goal: Improve Fiscal Stewardship
ROI: Consistent policy interpretation across departments
Declarative Knowledge

201-C: Benefits Screener Agent

⏱️ 30 mins

Persona: Constituent

Build a declarative knowledge agent with eligibility rules written directly into the instructions, no upload or web grounding required.

  • Use instruction-based eligibility logic for benefit screening
  • Guide people to programs and next steps
  • Keep the lab simple, contained, and easy to demo
Strategic Goal: Increase Benefit Uptake
ROI: More residents connected to benefits they qualify for

🏗️ Copilot 301

Copilot Studio labs for multi-turn routing, escalation, and grounded public information.

301-1: Constituent Services Agent

⏱️ 30 mins

Persona: Constituent

Build a multi-intent routing agent grounded on a city services directory and FAQ so people can get routed correctly across permits, utilities, events, and complaints.

  • Route to the right service path with clear escalation points
  • Provide relevant information from grounded service content
  • Reduce routine calls and emails through better self-service
Strategic Goal: Improve Constituent Experience
ROI: 40-60% reduction in routine phone and email inquiries

301-2: Case Triage Agent

⏱️ 30-35 mins

Persona: Case Worker or Service Staff

Build a multi-turn intake and escalation agent that captures case details, applies escalation logic, and recommends the right next step.

  • Gather case type, urgency, prior contact, and relevant details
  • Explicitly test when the agent should hand off to a human
  • Produce a structured case summary and escalation recommendation
Strategic Goal: Reduce Case Backlogs
ROI: Faster triage, consistent escalation, lower backlog age

301-3: Public Information Agent

⏱️ 30 mins

Persona: Constituent

Build a multi-source public information agent covering trash and recycling schedules, city events, utility billing basics, office hours, and contacts.

  • Use shipped city services content for an immediate build
  • Structure topic handling across common public information needs
  • Improve after-hours access without requiring a phone call
Strategic Goal: Improve Constituent Experience
ROI: Residents get answers 24/7 without calling city hall
