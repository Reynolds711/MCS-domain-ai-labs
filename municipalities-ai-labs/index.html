Welcome to Small Municipalities AI Labs

Learn to build declarative AI agents designed for small city and town governments. These hands-on labs guide you through creating practical solutions for constituent services, staff operations, and public information—all optimized for limited budgets and small teams.

Consulting Framework

Each lab answers three critical questions:

  • Who is the customer? Citizens, city staff, or elected officials
  • What repetitive, high-volume problem exists? Clear service gaps
  • What agent can remove friction using existing data? Low-cost solutions
💡 Why Municipalities?

Small cities (pop. 10,000-50,000) face unique challenges: limited IT budgets, small staff, high constituent expectations. AI agents can democratize access to government services without requiring expensive infrastructure or additional headcount.

🏛️ Civic AI Considerations

Government AI agents require special attention to:

  • Accessibility: Must serve all residents regardless of technology access
  • Transparency: Clearly disclose when AI is being used
  • Accuracy: Government information must be current and correct
  • Privacy: Never collect or store personal data without consent
  • Equity: Ensure agents serve all demographics and languages
  • Human escalation: Always provide path to human assistance

Measuring Agent Impact for Small Cities

Students learn to articulate value using this municipal framework:

Staff Time Saved

Hours per week freed from answering repetitive questions

Example: 15 hrs/week × $25/hr = $19,500/year saved

Constituent Satisfaction

24/7 access to information vs. office hours only

Example: 60% of inquiries resolved outside business hours

Service Equity

Equal access to information for all residents

Example: Multilingual support, after-hours access

Cost Avoidance

Alternative to hiring additional staff or expensive call centers

Example: Agent vs. $45K/year additional FTE

Real-World Municipality Use Cases

📋 Permit Applications

Problem: Citizens don't know which permit they need or what documents are required

Solution: Agent guides through permit selection and provides document checklist

Impact: Fewer incomplete applications, faster processing

🗑️ Waste Management

Problem: "When is my trash day?" is the #1 call to city hall

Solution: Agent provides schedule by address, recycling rules, bulk item pickup info

Impact: 70% reduction in waste-related calls

💧 Utility Billing

Problem: Residents have questions about bills, payment options, shut-off policies

Solution: Agent explains charges, payment plans, due dates

Impact: Reduced delinquencies, fewer disconnections

📅 City Events & Meetings

Problem: Low awareness of city council meetings, public hearings, events

Solution: Agent provides calendar, agendas, participation info

Impact: Increased civic engagement
