Welcome to State & Local Government AI Labs

This page blends your updated 101/201/301 outcomes with practical guided practice. Teams can run prompt labs, Studio Lite builds, and multi-turn orchestration scenarios in short facilitator-led sessions.

Consulting Framework

Each lab answers three critical questions:

  • Who is the customer? Constituents, frontline staff, or internal service teams
  • What repetitive, high-volume problem exists? Clear service, routing, or policy friction
  • What agent can remove friction using existing data? Grounded, practical solutions that improve service delivery
💡 Guided Practice Model

Each card includes a concise build challenge so participants actively practice configuration, testing, and refinement. Capture one before/after output and one time-to-resolution metric per lab.

🎯 Periodic Table Goal Alignment (SLG)

Goal set aligned to the SLG periodic table: Improve Fiscal Stewardship, Reduce Case Backlogs, Improve Constituent Experience, Increase Benefit Uptake, and Reduce Fraud & Risk.

🧠 Copilot 101

Prompt-only labs focused on quality, tone, and structure improvements in 15 minutes.

101-A: Constituent Communication

⏱️ 15 mins

Persona: City Hall Front Desk Staff

Draft a plain-language email response to a common resident question using clearer structure, tone, and accessible language.

  • Guided Practice: Start from a generic permit response prompt.
  • Guided Practice: Add constraints for plain language, structure, and contact next steps.
  • Guided Practice: Compare first versus final response for clarity and constituent experience.
Strategic Goal: Improve Constituent Experience
ROI: Faster response drafting, consistent resident communication

101-B: Council Meeting Agenda Builder

⏱️ 15 mins

Persona: City Manager or City Clerk

Turn raw department updates into a structured council meeting agenda with timing, action-item flags, and an executive summary.

  • Guided Practice: Start with unstructured department bullets.
  • Guided Practice: Add sections, estimated timing, and action item labels.
  • Guided Practice: Add executive summary formatting for elected leaders.
Strategic Goal: Improve Fiscal Stewardship
ROI: Agenda prep time cut from 2 hours to 20 minutes

Getting Started

Prerequisites

  • Access to Copilot Chat and Copilot Studio.
  • Sample civic policies, routing logic, and services content for test scenarios.
  • A facilitator with one note-taker per team for measuring outcomes.

Recommended Learning Path

  1. Run one 101 prompt lab to establish quality and tone controls.
  2. Run all 201 patterns (web, file, declarative knowledge).
  3. Run one 301 build with escalation testing and handoff review.
  4. Pick one extension and scope a pilot use case.

🏛️ Responsible Civic AI Considerations

  • Ensure plain-language accessibility for diverse constituent needs.
  • Use transparent escalation paths for high-risk or sensitive cases.
  • Keep policy-grounded responses current and source-backed.

Measuring State & Local Impact

Track value with simple metrics during pilots.

Resolution Speed

Faster response and routing across routine requests.

Example: Lower median time-to-answer on common inquiries

Routing Accuracy

Correct first-route assignment and fewer escalations due to misrouting.

Example: Fewer cases bounced between departments

Staff Capacity

More time for complex constituent needs and casework.

Example: Increased time available for high-touch work

Constituent Experience

Higher clarity and 24/7 self-service access for routine questions.

Example: Improved satisfaction with service interactions
