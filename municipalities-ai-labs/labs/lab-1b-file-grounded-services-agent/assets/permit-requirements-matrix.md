# City of Maplewood - Permit Requirements Matrix
**Effective January 2026**

---

## How to Use This Document
This matrix lists all permits issued by the City of Maplewood, including required documents, fees, and processing times. For questions, contact the Building Department at 555-0123.

---

## Building Permits

| Permit Type | Fee | Required Documents | Processing Time | Inspections Required |
|-------------|-----|-------------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| **New Construction - Residential** | $500 + $0.50/sq ft | Site plan, construction drawings, engineering (if required), contractor license | 10-15 business days | Foundation, framing, electrical, plumbing, final |
| **New Construction - Commercial** | $750 + $0.75/sq ft | Site plan, construction drawings, engineering, fire safety plan, contractor license | 15-20 business days | Multiple - see permit |
| **Addition (over 200 sq ft)** | $200 + $0.50/sq ft | Site plan, construction drawings, contractor license | 7-10 business days | Foundation (if applicable), framing, final |
| **Remodel (structural)** | $150 | Scope of work description, drawings, contractor license | 5-7 business days | Framing, final |
| **Remodel (non-structural)** | $75 | Scope of work description | 3-5 business days | Final only |
| **Deck** | $100 | Site plan showing setbacks, construction drawings, materials list | 5-7 business days | Footings, framing, final |
| **Shed (over 200 sq ft)** | $75 | Site plan showing setbacks, building specs | 3-5 business days | Final only |
| **Shed (120-200 sq ft)** | $50 | Site plan showing setbacks | 2-3 business days | Final only |
| **Shed (under 120 sq ft)** | No permit required | N/A | N/A | N/A |

### Building Permit Notes:
- All structural work requires a licensed contractor OR homeowner affidavit
- Work must begin within 6 months of permit issuance
- Permits expire after 12 months if work not completed
- Extensions available upon request ($25 fee)

## Inspection Scheduling

**Schedule Inspections:**
- Online: cityofmaplewood.gov/inspections
- Phone: 555-0124
- **Advance Notice Required:** 48 hours minimum

**Inspection Hours:**
- Monday - Friday: 8 AM - 4 PM
- Emergency inspections available for additional fee

**Inspection Results:**
- Approved: Work may continue
- Approved with Conditions: Minor corrections needed, reinspection not required
- Not Approved: Corrections required, reinspection required ($50 reinspection fee after 2nd failure)
