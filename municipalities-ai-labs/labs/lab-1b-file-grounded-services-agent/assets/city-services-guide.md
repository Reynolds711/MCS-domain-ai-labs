## Utilities Services

### Water Service

**New Service Connection**
- **Fee:** $75 connection fee + $50 deposit
- **Processing Time:** 3-5 business days
- **Requirements:**
  - Proof of property ownership or lease agreement
  - Valid government-issued ID
  - Completed application form
- **How to Apply:** Online at cityofmaplewood.gov/water or in person at City Hall, Room 102

**Water Rates (Effective January 2026)**
- Base fee: $15.50/month
- Usage rate: $4.25 per 1,000 gallons
- Sewer rate: $3.75 per 1,000 gallons (based on water usage)

**Billing Cycle:** Bills are issued monthly on the 15th, due on the 1st of the following month.

**Payment Options:**
- Online at cityofmaplewood.gov/pay
- Auto-pay enrollment available
- In person at City Hall (cash, check, credit card)
- Drop box located at City Hall entrance (checks only)
- By mail to: Maplewood Utilities, PO Box 1234, Maplewood, ST 12345

### Trash and Recycling Collection

**Residential Service**
- **Weekly trash collection:** Every Tuesday
- **Recycling collection:** Every other Tuesday (see schedule online)
- **Yard waste collection:** March through November, every Wednesday

**Cart Sizes and Monthly Rates:**
| Cart Size | Trash Fee | Recycling | Total Monthly |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| 35 gallon | $18.00 | Included | $18.00 |
| 65 gallon | $24.00 | Included | $24.00 |
| 95 gallon | $32.00 | Included | $32.00 |

**New Service Registration:**
- **Fee:** $25 one-time cart delivery fee
- **How to Register:** Call 555-0160 or visit cityofmaplewood.gov/trash
- **Cart Delivery:** Within 5 business days of registration

**Bulk Item Pickup:**
- Scheduled monthly on the last Saturday
- Call 555-0160 to schedule (48-hour advance notice required)
- Limit: 3 bulk items per household per pickup
- **Fee:** $15 per item for items over 50 lbs

**Recycling Accepted:**
- Paper, cardboard, newspaper
- Plastic containers #1-7
- Glass bottles and jars
- Aluminum and steel cans
- NO plastic bags, styrofoam, or food waste

### Stormwater Management

**Stormwater Fee:** $8.50/month per residential property
- Fee funds drainage maintenance, flood prevention, and water quality programs

**Report Drainage Issues:**
- Online: cityofmaplewood.gov/report
- Phone: 555-0150
- Response Time: Non-emergency issues addressed within 5 business days

## Public Works

### Street Maintenance

**Pothole Repair**
- **Report Online:** cityofmaplewood.gov/report-pothole
- **Report by Phone:** 555-0150
- **Response Time:**
  - Emergency (safety hazard): Within 24 hours
  - High priority (major road): 3-5 business days
  - Standard priority: 5-10 business days

**Street Sweeping Schedule:**
- Downtown: Every Monday
- Residential areas: Monthly (see schedule online)
- Request additional sweeping: Call 555-0150

### Sidewalk Maintenance

**City Responsibility:** Sidewalks adjacent to city property
**Property Owner Responsibility:** Sidewalks adjacent to private property

**Sidewalk Repair Program:**
- City offers 50/50 cost-sharing for residential sidewalk repairs
- Application deadline: March 31 annually
- Contact: Public Works at 555-0150

### Snow Removal

**Priority Routes:**
1. Emergency routes and main arterials
2. School zones and bus routes
3. Collector streets
4. Residential streets

**Residential Street Timeline:** Plowing begins after snowfall ends; residential streets cleared within 24 hours of snowfall cessation.

**Resident Responsibilities:**
- Clear sidewalks within 24 hours of snowfall ending
- Do not push snow into streets
- Keep fire hydrants accessible

**Parking During Snow Events:**
- Odd/even parking restrictions in effect during declared snow emergencies
- Monitor cityofmaplewood.gov/snow for declarations

### Traffic Signals and Signs

**Report Issues:**
- Malfunctioning signals: Call 555-0911 (emergency line)
- Damaged or missing signs: Call 555-0150 or report online
- Response time for signals: Within 4 hours
- Response time for signs: 1-3 business days

**Maplewood Community Center**
- Address: 100 Recreation Drive
- Hours: Mon-Fri 6 AM - 9 PM, Sat-Sun 8 AM - 6 PM
- Day pass: $8 adults, $5 seniors/youth
- Monthly membership: $45 individual, $75 family

**Maplewood Aquatic Center**
- Open: Memorial Day through Labor Day
- Hours: Daily 11 AM - 7 PM
- Day pass: $6 adults, $4 children under 12
- Season pass: $100 individual, $200 family

**Park Reservations**
- Pavilion rental: $50/day (residents), $75/day (non-residents)
- Athletic field rental: $25/hour
- Reserve online or call 555-0175
- Reservations accepted up to 6 months in advance

## Building and Zoning

### Permit Information

See the **Permit Requirements Matrix** document for complete permit details, fees, and required documents.

**General Information:**
- Permits required for most construction, renovation, and exterior changes
- Apply online at cityofmaplewood.gov/permits or in person at City Hall Room 201
- Inspections must be scheduled 48 hours in advance: Call 555-0124

### Zoning Information

**Zoning Map and Ordinance:** Available at cityofmaplewood.gov/zoning

**Common Zoning Questions:**
- Fence height limits: 4 feet in front yards, 6 feet in side/rear yards
- Accessory building limits: 200 sq ft without permit, larger requires permit
- Home occupation permits: Required for businesses operated from home
- Short-term rentals: Require special use permit in residential zones

**Variance Requests:**
- Application fee: $250
- Requires public hearing before Zoning Board
- Processing time: 6-8 weeks minimum
- Apply at City Hall Room 201

### Code Enforcement

**Report Violations:**
- Online: cityofmaplewood.gov/code-complaint
- Phone: 555-0125
- Anonymous reporting available

**Common Violations:**
- Tall grass/weeds (over 8 inches)
- Junk vehicles on property
- Unpermitted construction
- Sign violations

**Response Time:** Initial inspection within 5 business days of complaint

## Business Services

### Business Licenses

**New Business License**
- **Fee:** $75 annually
- **Home-based business:** $50 annually
- **Processing Time:** 5-7 business days
- **Renewal:** Due January 31 annually

**Required Documents:**
- Completed application
- State tax ID or proof of application
- Certificate of occupancy (for commercial locations)
- Home occupation permit (for home-based businesses)

### Special Event Permits

**Events on Public Property**
- **Application Deadline:** 30 days before event
- **Fee:** $50 for non-profit, $150 for commercial
- **Insurance Required:** $1 million liability minimum

**Events with Road Closures**
- **Additional Requirements:** Traffic control plan
- **Additional Fee:** $100
- **Application Deadline:** 60 days before event

### Economic Development Resources

**Starting a Business in Maplewood:**
1. Register with the State
2. Obtain City business license
3. Obtain required permits (building, sign, etc.)
4. Pass required inspections
5. Open for business!

**Free Resources:**
- Small Business Development Center consultations
- Site selection assistance
- Permit navigation assistance
- Contact Economic Development: 555-0140

## Public Safety

### Police Department

**Emergency:** 911
**Non-Emergency:** 555-0100

**Services:**
- Vacation house checks: Request online or call non-emergency
- Fingerprinting services: By appointment, $10 fee
- Accident reports: Available 3 business days after incident, $5 fee
- Background checks: $25 (for employment purposes)

### Fire Department

**Emergency:** 911
**Non-Emergency:** 555-0110

**Services:**
- Smoke detector installation: Free for seniors and disabled residents
- Fire extinguisher training: Monthly, free (register at cityofmaplewood.gov/fire)
- Car seat installation checks: By appointment, free
- Fire pit permits: $25 annual permit required

### Animal Control

**Contact:** 555-0115

**Services:**
- Stray animal pickup
- Barking dog complaints
- Wildlife concerns
- Pet licensing (required for dogs and cats)

**Pet Licensing:**
- **Fee:** $15/year (spayed/neutered), $30/year (unaltered)
- **Senior discount:** 50% off for residents 65+
- **Rabies vaccination required** for licensing
