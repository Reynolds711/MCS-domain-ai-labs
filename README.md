# MCS Domain AI Labs

Hands-on learning labs for building domain-specific AI agents with Microsoft Copilot Studio.

## Available Domains

- Manufacturing AI Labs: `Manu-MCS-labs/`
- K-12 Education AI Labs: `k12-education-ai-labs/`
- Small Municipalities AI Labs: `municipalities-ai-labs/`
- Higher Education AI Labs: `higher-education-ai-labs/`
- Nonprofit Operations AI Labs: `nonprofit-ai-labs/`
- Small & Medium Business (SMC) AI Labs: `smc-ai-labs/`

## SMC Domain (New)

The SMC track focuses on practical, high-ROI agent scenarios for small and medium businesses:

- Lab 1A: Web-Grounded SMB Growth Intelligence Agent
- Lab 1B: File-Grounded SMB Operations Knowledge Agent
- SMC Customer Service Triage Agent (30 mins)

Start here: [smc-ai-labs/index.html](smc-ai-labs/index.html)
