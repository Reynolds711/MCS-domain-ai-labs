# Elevate Youth Foundation
## Youth Safety Policies

---

## Purpose

The safety of the young people we serve is Elevate Youth Foundation's highest priority. These policies exist to:
- Protect students from harm
- Protect volunteers and staff from false allegations
- Create a culture of safety and accountability
- Ensure compliance with legal requirements

**All volunteers and staff must read, understand, and follow these policies.**

---

## Section 1: Mandatory Reporting

### 1.1 Legal Requirements

In our state, all adults who work with children in an official capacity are **mandatory reporters**. This means you are **legally required** to report suspected child abuse or neglect to the appropriate authorities.

**Failure to report is a crime** and may result in legal penalties as well as immediate removal from the volunteer program.

### 1.2 What Must Be Reported

You must report if you have **reasonable suspicion** of:

**Physical Abuse:**
- Unexplained bruises, burns, or injuries
- Injuries inconsistent with explanations given
- Injuries in unusual locations or patterns
- Child reports being hit, kicked, or physically harmed

**Neglect:**
- Child consistently hungry, without adequate food
- Child dressed inadequately for weather
- Child has poor hygiene that indicates lack of care
- Child left without adequate supervision
- Medical needs not being addressed

**Emotional Abuse:**
- Extreme behavior (aggressive or withdrawn)
- Adult belittling, threatening, or rejecting a child
- Child reports feeling worthless, unloved, or afraid

**Sexual Abuse:**
- Inappropriate sexual knowledge for age
- Sexualized behavior
- Physical signs of sexual abuse
- Child discloses sexual contact with an adult or older child

**Note:** You are reporting **suspicions**, not proof. Investigation is the job of trained professionals.

### 1.3 How to Report

**Step 1: Ensure Immediate Safety**
If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 first.

**Step 2: Document What You Observed**
Write down:
- Date and time
- What you observed OR exactly what the child said (use their words)
- Any visible injuries (location, appearance)
- Context (what led to the disclosure or observation)

**Step 3: Report to Elevate Program Director Immediately**
Contact: **Sarah Chen, Program Director**
- Phone: (555) 123-4568 (call or text)
- Email: sarah@elevateyouth.org
- In person if available

**Step 4: Follow Program Director's Guidance**
Sarah will:
- Assess the situation with you
- Make the report to Child Protective Services if required
- Ensure you are supported through the process
- Document the organizational response

**Step 5: Complete Incident Report**
Within 24 hours, complete an incident report in the Volunteer Portal.

### 1.4 What Happens After a Report

- Child Protective Services (CPS) investigates the report
- You may be contacted by investigators (Program Director will prepare you)
- The child's parents may or may not be informed, depending on the situation
- You will receive support from Elevate throughout the process

### 1.5 Confidentiality of Reports

- Your report is confidential—your identity is protected
- Do not discuss the report with other volunteers, parents, or the alleged abuser
- Do not investigate on your own
- Do not contact the child's family about the report

### 1.6 Key Principles

**"When in doubt, report."** It is NOT your job to determine if abuse occurred. That's what investigators are trained to do. Your job is to report your concerns.

**You will not get in trouble for a good-faith report.** Even if the investigation determines no abuse occurred, you are protected when you report in good faith.

**Do not promise confidentiality to children.** If a child says "Don't tell anyone, but..." you should say: "I care about you and want to help. If what you tell me is something that affects your safety, I might need to share it with someone who can help."

## Section 2: Appropriate Boundaries

### 2.1 Physical Contact Guidelines

**Appropriate Physical Contact:**
- Side hugs (initiated by adult or child)
- High-fives, fist bumps
- Pat on the shoulder or back
- Handshakes
- Holding a young child's hand for safety reasons

**Inappropriate Physical Contact:**
- Full frontal hugs (except brief greeting hugs in public settings)
- Kissing
- Lap sitting (for children over age 5)
- Tickling or wrestling
- Any contact with private areas
- Any contact that makes the child uncomfortable

**Key Principles:**
- Physical contact should be brief and appropriate
- Stop immediately if a child stiffens or pulls away
- Some children prefer no physical contact—respect this
- When in doubt, ask: "Is a high-five okay?"

### 2.2 One-on-One Interactions

**The Rule:** Never be alone with a child in an unobservable space.

**What This Means:**
- Meet in rooms with windows or open doors
- Stay in public, visible areas
- Ensure other adults are nearby
- If you must have a private conversation, do so in a visible location

**If You Find Yourself Alone:**
- Immediately move to a visible area
- Open doors, turn on lights
- Notify another adult of your location
- Document the situation

### 2.3 Transportation Policy

**Volunteers may NEVER transport students in personal vehicles.**

No exceptions. This protects both students and volunteers.

**If a child needs transportation:**
1. Stay with the child in a supervised area
2. Contact the parent/guardian through Elevate staff
3. If parent is unreachable, contact Program Coordinator
4. Stay with the child until an appropriate adult takes responsibility
5. Document the situation

**Rideshare/Taxi:** Volunteers may not arrange or pay for rideshare or taxi service for students.

### 2.4 Gift Giving

**Allowed:**
- Small items (under $10): stickers, bookmarks, school supplies
- Items provided by Elevate for incentives
- Food during program activities (following allergy guidelines)

**Requires Approval:**
- Gifts over $10 value
- Electronics of any value
- Clothing
- Cash or gift cards

**Never Allowed:**
- Gifts given secretly
- Gifts with personal contact information
- Gifts that create obligation or expectation
- Lavish or frequent gifts

**Key Principle:** Gift-giving should never feel secretive or create a special, private relationship.

### 2.5 Communication Boundaries

**All communication with students must go through official Elevate channels.**

**Approved Methods:**
- Elevate Connect (video platform)
- Elevate Messenger (in-app messaging)
- In-person during program activities

**Prohibited:**
- Personal phone calls or texts
- Personal email
- Social media (any platform)
- Personal video chat (FaceTime, Zoom, etc.)
- Gaming platforms, Discord, etc.

**If a Student Contacts You Through Personal Channels:**
1. Do not respond through that channel
2. Report to Program Coordinator immediately
3. Kindly redirect the student at your next session

### 2.6 Social Media Policy

**You May Not:**
- Follow, friend, or connect with students on any platform
- Share your social media handles with students
- Post photos of students on personal accounts
- Search for or view students' social media profiles
- Direct message students through any platform

**Official Elevate Social Media:**
- Photos at Elevate events are taken by authorized staff only
- Photo releases are obtained from parents
- You may be featured in official Elevate posts (with your permission)

## Section 3: Creating Safe Environments

### 3.1 Supervision Ratios

| Setting | Ratio | Notes |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Tutoring (general) | 1 adult : 4 students max | Open room, visible |
| Tutoring (intensive) | 1 adult : 2 students max | For students needing more support |
| Mentoring | 1 adult : 1 student | Observable location required |
| Field trips | 1 adult : 5 students | At least 2 adults total |
| Special events | 1 adult : 8 students | Depends on activity |

**Note:** At least one Elevate staff member must be on-site during all program activities.

### 3.2 Incident Response

**For ANY incident involving student safety:**

1. **Ensure immediate safety** - remove from harm, call 911 if emergency
2. **Stay calm** - children take cues from adult reactions
3. **Notify Elevate staff immediately** - find on-site staff or call Program Coordinator
4. **Document** - complete incident report within 24 hours
5. **Maintain confidentiality** - discuss only with appropriate Elevate staff

### 3.3 Medical Emergencies

**Life-Threatening Emergencies:**
1. Call 911 immediately
2. Stay with the child
3. Notify Elevate staff
4. Contact will be made with parents/guardians
5. Complete incident report

**Non-Life-Threatening Injuries:**
1. Provide basic first aid if trained
2. Notify Elevate staff on site
3. Contact parents/guardians through staff
4. Complete incident report

**You may NOT:**
- Provide medication (including over-the-counter)
- Make medical decisions
- Transport a child to receive medical care

### 3.4 Mental Health Concerns

**If a student expresses thoughts of self-harm or suicide:**

1. **Take it seriously** - never dismiss or minimize
2. **Stay calm** - your calm response helps the student
3. **Listen** - let them talk, don't try to solve it immediately
4. **Say:** "Thank you for trusting me with this. I want to help you. I need to share this with someone who can help."
5. **Do NOT promise confidentiality** for safety concerns
6. **Contact Program Director immediately:** Sarah Chen, (555) 123-4568
7. **Stay with the student** until a professional takes over
8. **Document** what the student said (use their words) in incident report

**Crisis Resources:**
- 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline: Call or text 988
- Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741

## Section 4: Special Situations

### 4.1 Students with Disabilities

- Follow any accommodation plans shared by parents or school
- Ask parents/guardians about communication preferences
- Treat students with respect and dignity
- Do not assume limitations—ask what support is helpful
- Report any accessibility concerns to Program Coordinator

### 4.2 Allergies and Medical Conditions

- Review any medical information shared during onboarding
- Ask before providing any food
- Know the location of EpiPens and first aid supplies
- Know which students have medical conditions
- When in doubt, provide no food and ask parents

### 4.3 Suspected Substance Use

If you suspect a student is under the influence:
1. Do not confront the student about suspected use
2. Ensure the student's immediate safety
3. Contact Elevate staff immediately
4. Stay with the student until staff takes over
5. Document in incident report

### 4.4 Weapons or Threats

If you become aware of a weapon or threat:
1. **Do not attempt to confiscate weapons**
2. Remove yourself and students from the area if safe to do so
3. Call 911 if there is immediate danger
4. Notify Elevate staff
5. Follow staff or law enforcement instructions
6. Document in incident report
