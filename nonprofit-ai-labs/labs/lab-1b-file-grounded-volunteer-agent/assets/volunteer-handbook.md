## Welcome to Elevate!

Thank you for joining Elevate Youth Foundation as a volunteer! You're joining a community of 200+ volunteers dedicated to helping K-12 students succeed through tutoring and mentorship. This handbook contains everything you need to know about being an Elevate volunteer.

**Our Mission:** To elevate educational outcomes for underserved K-12 students through high-quality tutoring and mentorship programs.

**Your Impact:** Last year, our volunteers provided 15,000+ hours of support, helping 500 students improve their grades and confidence.

## Section 1: Getting Started

### 1.1 Volunteer Requirements

Before you can begin volunteering with students, you must complete:

1. **Application & Interview** - Submit online application and complete phone/video interview
2. **Background Check** - National criminal background check (required for all volunteers)
3. **Reference Check** - We contact two professional/personal references
4. **Volunteer Orientation** - 2-hour in-person or virtual session
5. **Youth Safety Training** - Online module (approximately 1 hour)
6. **Program-Specific Training** - Training for your specific role (tutoring or mentoring)

**Timeline:** The complete onboarding process typically takes 2-3 weeks.

**Cost:** Background checks are provided at no cost to volunteers.

### 1.2 Background Check Requirements

All volunteers must pass a background check before working with students. Our background check includes:
- National criminal database search
- Sex offender registry check
- Social Security number verification

**Disqualifying offenses include:**
- Any felony conviction
- Crimes against children
- Sexual offenses
- Violent crimes
- Drug-related offenses (case-by-case review for non-felony)

Background checks are valid for two years. You will be notified when renewal is needed.

### 1.3 Training Requirements

**Volunteer Orientation (Required - 2 hours)**
- Elevate mission, values, and programs
- Volunteer expectations and policies
- Introduction to the students we serve
- Tour of facilities (in-person) or virtual walkthrough

**Youth Safety Training (Required - 1 hour online)**
- Recognizing signs of abuse and neglect
- Mandatory reporting requirements
- Appropriate boundaries with students
- Creating safe environments

**Program Training (Required - varies by role)**
- Tutoring volunteers: 3-hour tutoring methods training
- Mentoring volunteers: 4-hour mentoring fundamentals training

**Ongoing Training (Recommended)**
- Monthly volunteer workshops on various topics
- Annual refresher training (required)

## Section 2: Volunteer Expectations

### 2.1 Commitment Requirements

**Tutoring Volunteers:**
- Minimum 1-hour session per week
- Minimum 3-month commitment
- Same day/time each week preferred for student consistency

**Mentoring Volunteers:**
- Minimum 1-hour meeting per week (in-person or virtual as approved)
- Minimum 6-month commitment
- One-to-one mentoring with assigned student

### 2.2 Attendance & Reliability

Students depend on you. Consistent attendance is essential.

**Attendance Expectations:**
- Arrive on time for scheduled sessions
- Stay for the full session duration
- Notify us at least 24 hours in advance if you cannot attend

**Absence Notification Process:**
1. Log into the Volunteer Portal at volunteer.elevateyouth.org
2. Click "Report Absence" at least 24 hours before your session
3. Provide reason and whether you need a substitute
4. For same-day emergencies, call the Program Coordinator at (555) 123-4567

**Excessive Absences:**
- More than 2 unexcused absences may result in removal from the program
- Three consecutive missed sessions without notification will result in removal

### 2.3 Dress Code

Volunteers should dress in a manner appropriate for a school environment:
- Casual business attire or neat casual clothing
- No clothing with offensive graphics or language
- Closed-toe shoes recommended
- Elevate volunteer t-shirt provided and encouraged

### 2.4 Professional Conduct

As an Elevate volunteer, you represent our organization. Please:
- Treat all students, staff, and fellow volunteers with respect
- Use appropriate language at all times
- Maintain a positive, encouraging attitude
- Follow the direction of school staff when on school premises
- Report any concerns to Elevate staff promptly

## Section 3: Working with Students

### 3.1 Appropriate Boundaries

Maintaining appropriate boundaries protects both students and volunteers.

**Physical Contact:**
- Side hugs, high-fives, and fist bumps are acceptable
- Avoid prolonged physical contact
- Never be alone with a student in an unobservable space
- Follow the student's lead on physical contact (some may prefer no contact)

**Communication Boundaries:**
- All communication with students must go through official Elevate channels
- Use the Elevate messaging platform for any student communication
- Personal phone numbers, email, and social media are NOT to be shared

**Gift Giving:**
- Small items (stickers, bookmarks) are acceptable
- Gifts over $10 value must be approved by the Program Coordinator
- Never give gifts with your personal contact information

### 3.2 Social Media Policy

**Absolutely Prohibited:**
- Following, friending, or connecting with students on any personal social media
- Sharing personal social media handles with students
- Posting photos of students on personal social media
- Direct messaging students outside of Elevate platforms

**Official Elevate Social Media:**
- You may follow Elevate's official social media accounts
- Photos taken at Elevate events may be posted ONLY by authorized staff
- Photo releases are obtained from parents/guardians

**If a Student Requests to Connect:**
Politely decline and explain: "It's one of our program rules that we don't connect on social media, but I'm happy to chat with you during our sessions!"

### 3.3 Confidentiality

**You must keep confidential:**
- Student names and personal information
- Academic performance and challenges
- Family situations and background
- Anything a student shares with you in confidence (except safety concerns—see Section 5)

**You may discuss (in appropriate contexts):**
- General volunteer experiences (without identifying students)
- Program information that is publicly available
- Your own growth and learning as a volunteer

### 3.4 Transportation Policy

**Volunteers may NOT:**
- Transport students in personal vehicles under any circumstances
- Offer rides to students or their family members
- Meet students anywhere other than approved program locations

**If a student needs transportation:**
- Contact the Program Coordinator for assistance
- Stay with the student until an appropriate adult arrives
- Never leave a student unsupervised

## Section 4: Tutoring Program Specifics

### 4.1 Session Structure

Tutoring sessions follow this recommended structure:
1. **Check-in (5 minutes):** Greet student, ask about their week
2. **Goal Setting (5 minutes):** What will we work on today?
3. **Active Tutoring (40 minutes):** Work on assignments, concepts
4. **Wrap-up (10 minutes):** Review progress, set goals for next time

### 4.2 Academic Support Guidelines

**Do:**
- Help students understand concepts
- Guide students through problem-solving processes
- Encourage students to try before providing answers
- Celebrate effort and progress

**Don't:**
- Complete assignments for students
- Give answers without explanation
- Do work that will be graded as the student's own
- Criticize teachers or school staff

### 4.3 Working with Multiple Students

Some tutoring sessions involve small groups (2-4 students).

**Group Supervision Ratio:**
- Maximum 4 students per volunteer for homework help
- Maximum 2 students per volunteer for intensive tutoring
- Always ensure all students are visible and engaged

## Section 5: Safety Policies

### 5.1 Mandatory Reporting

As a volunteer working with children, you are a **mandatory reporter** in our state. This means you are legally required to report suspected child abuse or neglect.

**You MUST report if you:**
- Observe signs of physical abuse (unexplained injuries, bruises)
- Observe signs of neglect (poor hygiene, hunger, inadequate clothing)
- Are told by a child about abuse or neglect
- Observe signs of emotional abuse
- Suspect sexual abuse

**How to Report:**
1. **Do NOT investigate** - that is not your role
2. **Document** what you observed or what the child said (exact words if possible)
3. **Report immediately** to the Program Director: Sarah Chen, (555) 123-4568, sarah@elevateyouth.org
4. The Program Director will guide next steps, which may include a report to Child Protective Services

**Remember:** You are required to report **suspicions**, not proof. "When in doubt, report."

### 5.2 Emergency Procedures

**Medical Emergency:**
1. Call 911 immediately for life-threatening situations
2. Notify school staff if on school premises
3. Stay with the student
4. Contact Program Coordinator as soon as possible
5. Complete incident report within 24 hours

**Non-Emergency Incidents:**
1. Address immediate safety concerns
2. Notify Program Coordinator within 4 hours
3. Complete incident report within 24 hours

**Incident Report Process:**
- Log into Volunteer Portal
- Click "Report Incident"
- Provide factual description of what occurred
- Do not include speculation or opinions

### 5.3 Supervision Requirements

**Volunteers must never be alone with a student in an unobservable space.**

This means:
- Doors remain open
- Work in visible areas (glass windows, open rooms)
- At least one other adult should be nearby

**If you find yourself alone with a student:**
- Move to a visible, public area immediately
- Notify another adult of your location
- Document the situation

- Email: programcoordinator@elevateyouth.org
- Phone: (555) 123-4567 (Monday-Friday, 9 AM - 5 PM)

### 6.2 Communicating with Parents/Guardians

**General Rule:** Direct communication with parents/guardians goes through Elevate staff.
