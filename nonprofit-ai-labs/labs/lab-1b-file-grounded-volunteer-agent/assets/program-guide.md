# Elevate Youth Foundation
## STEM Mentorship Program Guide

---

## Program Overview

The STEM Mentorship Program connects adult professionals with middle and high school students interested in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics careers. Through one-on-one mentoring relationships, students gain exposure to STEM careers, develop academic skills, and build confidence.

**Program Goals:**
- Expose underserved students to STEM career possibilities
- Provide academic support in math and science subjects
- Develop students' problem-solving and critical thinking skills
- Build mentoring relationships that support students' long-term success

**Target Students:**
- Grades 6-12
- Interest in STEM subjects or careers
- Referrals from school counselors, teachers, or self-referral
- Priority given to students from underrepresented backgrounds in STEM

---

## Mentor Requirements

### Who Can Be a STEM Mentor?

**Required:**
- 21 years of age or older
- Work experience or education in a STEM field
- Ability to commit to 6+ months of mentoring
- Pass background check and complete training
- Reliable transportation to meeting locations

**Preferred:**
- Currently working in STEM profession
- Experience working with youth
- Bilingual (Spanish/English)

### Time Commitment

**Weekly Meetings:**
- 1 hour per week (minimum)
- Same day/time each week recommended for consistency
- Can be in-person or virtual (based on program track—see below)

**Additional Time:**
- Monthly mentor networking events (optional but encouraged)
- Quarterly program workshops (required)
- Annual mentor training refresher (required)

---

## Program Tracks

### Track A: In-Person Mentoring

**Meeting Locations:**
- School partner sites during after-school hours
- Elevate Community Center (Saturday mornings)
- Pre-approved public locations (libraries, community centers)

**Meeting Requirements:**
- Must be in observable public spaces
- No meetings at private homes
- No off-site activities without advance approval and parent permission

### Track B: Virtual Mentoring

**Platform:** Elevate Connect (secure video platform)

**Requirements:**
- All sessions conducted through Elevate Connect (no personal Zoom, FaceTime, etc.)
- Sessions are recorded for safety and quality assurance
- Students must have parent permission for virtual mentoring
- Cameras on for both mentor and student during sessions

**When Virtual is Required:**
- Geographic distance
- Student transportation limitations
- Health/safety considerations
- Weather-related school closures

## Mentor-Mentee Matching

### How Matching Works

1. **Application:** Student completes application with interests, goals, availability
2. **Interview:** Program staff interviews student and parent/guardian
3. **Matching:** Staff matches based on:
   - STEM interest areas (biology, computer science, engineering, etc.)
   - Schedule compatibility
   - Personality and communication style
   - Mentor preferences and expertise
4. **Introduction:** Supervised introduction meeting with mentor, student, and parent
5. **Launch:** First solo session with mentor

### Re-Matching

Sometimes matches don't work out. If you or your mentee feel the match isn't right:
- Speak with your Program Coordinator
- Re-matching is available with no judgment
- Students and mentors are not told the reason for re-matching requests

## Session Structure

### Recommended Session Format (60 minutes)

| Time | Activity | Description |
|------|----------|-------------|
| 0-10 min | Check-In | How's your week? School? Life? Build relationship. |
| 10-15 min | Goal Review | Check progress on goals from last session |
| 15-45 min | Core Activity | STEM exploration, academic help, career discussion |
| 45-55 min | Goal Setting | What will you work on before next session? |
| 55-60 min | Wrap-Up | Confirm next meeting, positive send-off |

### Activity Ideas by Focus Area

**Academic Support:**
- Homework help in math/science
- Study skills and test preparation
- Science fair project guidance
- College application support (seniors)

**Career Exploration:**
- Share your STEM career journey
- Virtual workplace tour
- Introduction to your colleagues (with approval)
- Resume and interview practice (high school students)

**Hands-On STEM:**
- Simple experiments or projects
- Coding activities (Scratch, Python basics)
- Engineering challenges (bridge building, etc.)
- Use activity kits available from Elevate

**Life Skills:**
- Time management
- Communication skills
- Problem-solving approaches
- Growth mindset development

## Communication Guidelines

### Approved Communication Methods

| Method | When to Use | Notes |
|--------|-------------|-------|
| Elevate Connect (video) | Virtual sessions | Sessions recorded |
| Elevate Messenger | Between-session check-ins | Messages visible to staff |
| Volunteer Portal | Scheduling, questions | For mentor-staff communication |
| Email (official only) | When directed by staff | Never personal email |

### Prohibited Communication

- Personal cell phone calls or texts with students
- Personal email with students
- Social media connection of any kind
- Messaging apps (WhatsApp, Snapchat, etc.)
- Video calls outside Elevate Connect

**If a student contacts you through unauthorized channels:**
1. Do not respond through that channel
2. Notify your Program Coordinator immediately
3. Remind student at next session about approved communication methods

### Communication with Parents/Guardians

- Parents receive monthly progress updates from Elevate staff
- You may briefly greet parents at pickup/drop-off
- Direct questions about the student's progress to your Program Coordinator
- Do not exchange personal contact information with parents

## Attendance & Scheduling

### Scheduling Your Sessions

**Initial Schedule:**
- Set your recurring day/time with your Program Coordinator
- Consistency is key for student trust and progress

**Changing Your Schedule:**
- Contact Program Coordinator at least 1 week in advance
- Permanent changes require discussion with student and parent

### Absence Policy

**Planned Absences:**
- Report through Volunteer Portal at least 24 hours in advance
- Suggest alternative times if possible
- Your student will be notified by staff

**Emergency Absences:**
- Call Program Coordinator: (555) 123-4567
- Follow up with Volunteer Portal report

**Student Absences:**
- You will be notified by staff if your student cancels
- Two consecutive student absences trigger staff follow-up

**Excessive Absences:**
- More than 2 mentor absences per quarter requires a conversation with Program Director
- More than 3 consecutive absences may result in match reassignment

## Progress Tracking

### Monthly Progress Notes

Mentors submit brief monthly progress notes through the Volunteer Portal.

**Include:**
- Sessions completed this month
- Topics/activities covered
- Student engagement level (scale 1-5)
- Goals in progress
- Concerns or support needed

**Due:** 5th of each month for the previous month

### Goal Setting

Each mentor-mentee pair sets 2-3 goals per semester:

**Academic Goals Examples:**
- Improve math grade from C to B
- Complete science fair project
- Submit 3 college applications

**STEM Exploration Goals Examples:**
- Research 3 STEM careers of interest
- Complete one coding project
- Attend one STEM event or workshop

**Personal Development Goals Examples:**
- Improve time management skills
- Practice public speaking
- Build confidence in asking for help

## Supervision & Support

### Program Coordinator Support

Your Program Coordinator is your primary support. They will:
- Conduct monthly check-ins (more frequent for new mentors)
- Observe sessions periodically (announced)
- Respond to questions within 24 hours
- Connect you with resources and support

### Session Observation

All mentoring sessions in the In-Person Track are in observable locations. Staff may drop in to observe sessions. Virtual sessions are recorded for review.

**Observation Purpose:**
- Ensure student safety
- Provide coaching and feedback
- Quality assurance

### Mentor Networking

**Monthly Mentor Meetups:**
- First Thursday of each month, 6:00-7:30 PM
- Share experiences and learn from fellow mentors
- Optional but encouraged

**Mentor Buddy Program:**
- New mentors paired with experienced mentors
- Check-ins during first 3 months
- Ask questions and get peer support

## Ending a Mentoring Relationship

### Planned Transitions

When a mentoring relationship ends (graduation, relocation, etc.):
1. Notify Program Coordinator at least 4 weeks in advance
2. Plan closure sessions with your mentee
3. Participate in transition process (introduce new mentor if applicable)
4. Complete exit reflection survey

### Unplanned Endings

If you need to end the relationship unexpectedly:
1. Contact Program Coordinator immediately
2. Elevate staff will manage communication with student/family
3. Provide any necessary documentation

**Student Welfare:** Even in difficult endings, student welfare is the priority. Staff will ensure appropriate closure for the student.
