Welcome to Manufacturing AI Labs

Learn to build declarative AI agents tailored for manufacturing industry challenges. These hands-on labs guide you through real-world scenarios including equipment maintenance, production optimization, quality control, and more.

Consulting Framework

Each lab answers three critical questions:

  • Who is the customer? Operators, supervisors, engineers, or managers
  • What repetitive, high-volume problem exists? Clear pain points
  • What agent can remove friction using existing data? Measurable solutions
